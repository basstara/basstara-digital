Privacy Policy

Document Status: Effective for the current release version.

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which PT Basstara Digital Indonesia (hereinafter referred to as "Basstara Digital", "Company", "We", "Our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User", "You") of the Mastering Pro mobile application ("App"). This document is designed to strictly comply with global data protection regulations and the developer policies of the respective App Distribution Platforms where this App is downloaded.

1. DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE

1.1. Offline Processing: This App is designed to operate independently on the User's hardware. All forms of digital signal processing (DSP), audio rendering, and frequency manipulation are executed locally (on-device).

1.2. Non-Personal Data: Information that cannot independently be used to specifically identify an individual, such as device specifications, operating systems, and anonymous advertising identifiers.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We implement the principle of data minimization. We do not collect Personally Identifiable Information (such as name, email, or contacts) as the App does not require account registration.

2.1. User Data (Audio & Media)

We explicitly state that the App never transmits, uploads, records, or stores User audio files to any internal or external servers. The intellectual property rights and privacy over all audio materials remain fully with the User.

2.2. Automatically Collected Data (Third Parties)

Through the integration of advertising and billing Software Development Kits (SDKs), our systems may automatically collect Non-Personal Data, which includes:

  • Device Identifiers (Advertising ID / Android ID).
  • Anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) Addresses for service localization purposes.
  • Purchase history, subscription status, and transaction tokens for premium feature fulfillment.

2.3. In-App Purchases & Subscription (Offline Billing)

The App offers premium features through In-App Purchases. Payment processing is securely handled by the official Billing Infrastructure of the platform from which you downloaded this App (e.g., Google Play, Xiaomi GetApps, Samsung Galaxy Store, or other official partners). We do not collect, transmit, or store your credit card or financial data on our servers. Subscription verification is processed locally on-device via official platform tokens to ensure uninterrupted offline access to premium tools.

3. USE OF THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1. Ad Serving & Analytics: The App is ad-supported, facilitated by Google AdMob. AdMob utilizes the Advertising ID to serve relevant advertisements in accordance with global safety guidelines. We recommend Users review the Google Privacy Policy regarding data management.

3.2. Billing Infrastructure: The App utilizes the official billing APIs provided by the respective distribution platforms. Data processing related to your financial transactions is subject to the Privacy Policy of the specific app store you use.

4. USER RIGHTS AND CONTROL

We respect the User's autonomy over their data. Users have full rights to exercise control over ad tracking via their device operating system settings:

  • Users may reset their Advertising ID at any time.
  • Users may opt-out of ad personalization by navigating to their device's Privacy & Ads settings.

5. APP PERMISSIONS

The App only requests permissions that are technically absolute necessities for the execution of core features:

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO: This access is strictly limited solely to loading audio files from internal storage into the App's processing module.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (on relevant Android versions): Used exclusively to save (export) the final mastered audio results back to the User's local storage directory.

6. DATA SECURITY

We apply reasonable technical and organizational security standards to protect your information. Due to the absence of audio data transmission to the internet, the risk of interception (Man-in-the-Middle attacks) against your audio artwork has been mitigated since the software architecture phase (Privacy by Design).

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA)

This App is categorized as a professional utility tool and does not target or knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If there is an indication of data collection without verifiable parental consent, such data will be immediately purged from our third-party logs.

8. POLICY REVISIONS

PT Basstara Digital Indonesia reserves the prerogative to update, modify, or amend this Privacy Policy at any time without individual notice, to adapt to the dynamics of global technology laws. The revised policy will become effective immediately upon publication on our official repository.

9. CONTACT INFORMATION

Any legal correspondence, inquiries, or complaints related to the execution of this Privacy Policy can be directed to the Developer's official email address provided on the respective App Store distribution page where the App was downloaded.
