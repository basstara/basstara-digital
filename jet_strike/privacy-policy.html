Privacy Policy

Application: Jet Strike (com.jet.strike)
Developer: Basstara Digital Indonesia
Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Basstara Digital Indonesia ("we," "our," or "us") built the mobile game Jet Strike as a free-to-play application supported by optional advertisements and in-app purchases. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Jet Strike does not require account registration, login, or submission of personal information such as your name, address, or phone number directly to us. We do not operate external player servers to collect or store your personal data.

Game progress—including high scores, virtual coins, unlocked aircraft and arenas, hangar upgrades, and audio preferences—is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the application or clearing its app data may permanently delete local progress, except for non-consumable purchases linked to your Google Play account.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To display advertisements, manage user consent, and process in-app purchases, the application integrates official Google services. These third-party providers may automatically collect and process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to serve interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect and process your Android Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location (derived from IP), app interaction events, and crash/performance diagnostics to deliver and measure advertisements. (Google Privacy Policy | How Google Uses Ad Data)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to request and store advertising consent preferences where required by applicable regional privacy regulations (such as EEA, UK, and Switzerland).
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Used to securely process optional digital purchases (such as VIP Pass, Remove Ads, and virtual coin packs) and to restore permanent purchases. All payment methods and billing transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play; we never receive or store your credit card or banking details. (Google Play Terms of Service)

3. Offline Functionality

Core gameplay is available offline without an internet connection. An internet connection is only required to load advertisements, complete or restore Google Play in-app purchases, and update regional consent preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Jet Strike is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party services used in the app, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

5. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

  • Ad Personalization: You can limit or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Regional Consent Options: Where required by law, you can review or update your ad consent choices directly inside the app's Shop menu.
  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the application.

6. Data Security

All network communications with Google AdMob, UMP, and Google Play Billing are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. While no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, we rely on trusted Google platform infrastructure to protect transaction and diagnostic data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in features, legal requirements, or third-party SDK updates. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
