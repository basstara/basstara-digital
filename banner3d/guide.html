How to Use

Mastering the Precision Slider Engine

1. Formations & Device Models

Start by selecting how many devices you want to display (from 1 to 7 screens). Choose your preferred device model (Notch Style, Punch Hole, Minimalist, etc.) and a 3D layout style that fits your design needs.

2. Importing Images (Screenshots)

Go to the Images tab. You can upload screenshots individually for each screen, or use the Master Sync feature (Premium) to upload a single image that automatically perfectly fills all active screens in the formation.

3. 3D Positional Tuning (Sliders)

Use the Precision Sliders in the Design tab to manipulate the 3D space. You can rotate (X, Y, Z axis), move, and scale the entire device formation. Tap on the numerical values to manually type in exact coordinates for absolute precision.

4. Virtual Environment & Text

Add depth to your banner by selecting a High-Resolution Virtual Background (or import your own custom image). You can also enable Dynamic Text Overlay, pick a color using the Spectrum Slider, and adjust its Z-Index to place it behind or in front of the devices.

5. High-Resolution Export

Once you are satisfied with the composition, tap the Export tab. The app's rendering engine will instantly generate a 100% distortion-free, high-definition PNG file saved directly to your device's gallery.
